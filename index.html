      How do I get my Spotify file?
      1. Open Spotify’s privacy page and sign in.
      2. Tick Extended streaming history and tap Request data. Spotify emails you when it’s ready, usually within a few days.
      3. Open the email, download the zip and open it. Pick the files whose names start with Streaming_History.
      Last.fm Keeps up with what you play on Spotify
      How does Last.fm work?
      1. Create a free account at last.fm.
      2. In Last.fm settings, open Applications and connect Spotify. From then on every song you play is recorded.
      3. Type your username here and tap Connect.
